Frequently Asked Questions

How do I unlock all lessons?

Tap on any locked lesson or phrase category and you'll be prompted to purchase Full Access. This is a one-time purchase that unlocks all current and future content.

I purchased Full Access but content is still locked

Go to Profile → Settings → Restore Purchases. This will re-verify your purchase with Apple and unlock all content. If the issue persists, please contact us at alex.d.code@icloud.com with your Apple order number.

Can I use the app offline?

Yes — all lesson content, exercises, and phrases are stored locally on your device. No internet connection is required after the initial download.

How do I change the app language?

The app supports English, Russian, and Ukrainian interface languages. To change it, go to Profile → Language and select your preferred language.

How do I switch between light and dark mode?

Go to Profile → Settings → Theme and choose between Auto (follows your device setting), Light, or Dark.

My progress was reset. What happened?

Progress is stored locally on your device. If you deleted and reinstalled the app, progress may have been lost unless you have an iCloud backup. Unfortunately, we cannot restore progress remotely as we don't store any data on our servers.

I found an error in a lesson or translation

We appreciate the feedback! Please email us at alex.d.code@icloud.com with the details of the error (lesson name, what seems incorrect, and what you think the correct version should be).

Can I request new content or features?

Absolutely — we'd love to hear from you. Send your suggestions to alex.d.code@icloud.com.
